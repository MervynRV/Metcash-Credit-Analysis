# Metcash Limited (ASX: MTS): Credit Analysis of a $500m Loan Facility

**Question:** Metcash has asked Commonwealth Bank for a $500 million long-term loan.
As CBA's credit analyst, should the board approve it?

**Answer:** Approve with conditions. Metcash can carry the loan, but there is little
headroom if earnings fall.

## What the model does
Built in Excel from Metcash's FY2021–FY2025 annual reports (year end 30 April).

1. **Raw data:** income statement, balance sheet (FY20–FY25) and cash flow,
   with check rows that confirm the balance sheet balances every year
2. **Six analyst adjustments**
3. **Ratio analysis:** profitability, liquidity and solvency, reported vs adjusted
4. **Loan stress test:** FY25 pro forma with the $500m loan, base and downside cases

## The six adjustments
| # | Adjustment | Why it matters to a lender |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remove significant items | "One-off" costs appeared in every year, so core earnings were re-tested |
| 2 | Charge-through sales | Metcash carries the credit risk on about $2.2bn of sales not in revenue |
| 3 | Amex charge card offset | About $261m of retailer balances come back to Metcash if a store defaults |
| 4 | Adjusted debt | Leases, put options and off-balance-sheet guarantees added to borrowings |
| 5 | Tangible net worth | Goodwill and deferred tax assets removed from equity |
| 6 | Organic growth | Superior Foods acquisition stripped out of FY25 |

## Key findings (FY25)
| Metric | Reported | Adjusted |
|---|---|---|
| Net debt | $577m | $1,760m |
| Net debt / EBITDA | 0.96x | 2.35x |
| Equity cushion | $1,637m | $54m tangible |
| FY25 revenue growth | 8.9% | 1.0% organic |

- Interest cover fell from 9.4x (FY21) to 4.2x (FY25)
- Free cash flow did not cover dividends in FY22 and FY23

## Stress test
| | FY25 actual | + $500m loan | + loan, EBIT −20% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interest cover | 4.15x | 3.60x | 2.88x |
| Net debt / EBITDA | 2.35x | 2.83x | 3.29x |

EBIT only has to fall **7.9%** before adjusted leverage breaks 3.0x.

## Recommended loan conditions
- Staged drawdown
- Covenants: net debt / EBITDA ≤ 3.0x, interest cover ≥ 3.0x
- Dividend restriction above 2.75x leverage
- Cap on further debt-funded acquisitions
